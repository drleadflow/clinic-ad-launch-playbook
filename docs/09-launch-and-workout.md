# 09 · Launch document and workout plan (1 hour)

Launch document sections: offer; audience and segments; positioning stack; slate (12 cells, both sizes); campaign structure (objective, ad sets, budgets, exclusions, optimisation event on the real conversion, Standard Enhancements off); test design; success metrics (week-1 CPL target, kill line, true north = booked appointments); blockers named (price approval, pixel, dry-run flags, calendar, phone mismatch); 72-hour hands-off; owner approval line.

Price and deposit (mid-ticket offers): show the price on the creative and make the small refundable or credited deposit the action ("Reserve with $X, credited to your session"). Hiding the price buys volume and loses show rate. When the clinic can take a deposit online, test two ad sets at equal budget: instant form (more leads, setter qualifies) against deposit-first (ad → page → calendar with payment, pixel event on the paid deposit). Judge both on cost per paid deposit; cost per lead will always flatter the form.

Workout plan, pre-written before launch:

| Week | Hands on | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (72 h) | none | learning phase |
| 1 | day 4 and 7 | judge CPL on the real conversion; hold under target; pause the ad, not the ad set, over the kill line |
| 2 | twice | kill the bottom half; raise the winner 20 to 30%, never double; retargeting goes live |
| 3 | twice | refresh: three big swings off the winning family; replace fallback customer language with real inbox lines |
| 4 | retro | 30-day scorecard, closed-won by creative, what enters the pattern library |

Standing rules: refresh every 8 to 12 days before frequency passes 3; CTR under 2.5% is fatigue, not budget; one test angle always queued; never one campaign carrying a client.

Pre-flight before activating: everything paused, destination returns 200 and a test lead lands, UTMs on every ad, both sizes mapped to placements, budget math matches the doc, exclusions attached, naming convention consistent, no treatment name in the pixel URL, no clinical field in the lead form unless a BAA exists.
