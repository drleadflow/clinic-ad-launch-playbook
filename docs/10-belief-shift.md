# 14 · Belief shift (the Shift Framework pass)

**When:** after the first roster (phase 3) and before wave 2 production. **Time:** 1 hour from memory, 2 with an interview. **Output:** `13-shift-framework.md` (and a Word version if the client wants one).

Angle banks built from research cover product and proof. They miss the beliefs that stop a booking: time, money, who to trust, doing it alone, and the identity she is embarrassed to have. This pass writes those down once and turns each into a headline.

## Inputs
Client sheet, research VOC lines, the provider's own words (video transcript, reviews), account history. If the subject is a provider, ask for one 60-second voice note: why she works the way she does.

## Steps
1. **Avatar and villain.** The avatar in her sentences. The villain is a business model, never a competitor by name.
2. **Two identities.** Name the before (embarrassing, specific) and the after (proud, specific). Short and memorable.
3. **Core 6.** Old belief → new belief → proof on hand, one row each: core method, real problem, time, money, method, help. If proof is missing, the row is a placeholder, not a claim.
4. **Paid kit.** 4 hooks (each tagged to a belief), 3 bodies, 2 CTAs, a 6-cell test matrix, one 15-second middle-of-funnel script per belief plus personal story, testimonial, cost of inaction.
5. **Tag PROVIDER vs PRACTICE.** Provider scripts use her name and lines; practice scripts re-shoot with anyone later.
6. **Turn each belief into a static headline** in two visual systems (the skeleton and one editorial style). That is wave 2.

## Gate
Every hook speaks to a group, never "you" plus a body attribute. Zero em dashes. No invented stats or stories. Placeholders marked.
