# 01 · Research (2 to 3 hours)

Research is reading what already runs, not brainstorming. Do it in this order.

0. **Your own winners, at ad level.** If the account already runs a live offer, break it down by ad before anything else: spend share, cost per lead on the real conversion, CTR, saves. When one ad carries most of the spend at a good cost, read its layout element by element (headline, price shape, anchor, bonus, person, CTA, word count) and treat that as the starting template for the next offer. Note which siblings never got delivery: they were not tested, so the evidence is for the format, not the details.
1. **Your own category rules.** If you keep a vault of winning ads, read the category first: the price shapes, the destinations, the longest runners. If you do not, start one after this launch.
2. **Meta Ad Library, three sweeps.** (a) the offer keyword + the metro, (b) national chains in the category, (c) same-avatar adjacent offers (for body contouring: cryo, red light, Emsculpt; for resurfacing: peels, microneedling, IPL, tox bundles). Log 20+ ads: advertiser, hook, offer shape, format, CTA, destination, days running, number of variants. **Days running and variant count are the winner signals.** In the browser, sort by impressions instead of recency: `facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=US&q=%22<term>%22&search_type=keyword_exact_phrase&media_type=all&sort_data[mode]=total_impressions&sort_data[direction]=desc`. Exact phrase matters for device names; an unordered search matches the words separately. For video, use the Apify recipe in `tools/adlibrary-scrape.md`; it returns the video files and preview images.
3. **Account history.** The client's last 90 days at ad level, ranked by cost per lead on the real conversion. Anything under your "excellent" band is a proven reusable and goes in the remix pack.
4. **Customer language.** Ten lines minimum in the patient's own words. Sources: the client's inbox and reviews; for a new offer with no history, Reddit threads (recipe in `tools/reddit-voc.md`), RealSelf, competitor ad comments. Capture verbatim. Replace with real inbox lines at the week-3 refresh.
5. **The competitor with your device.** Search the device name. If a distributor or a pop-up runs it in your city, that is your first positioning problem and your first angle ("same device, the difference is who assesses you").

Output: ICP and the beliefs she holds, positioning stack (mechanism → honesty → identity → offer), open lanes, competitor table, customer-language bank, segments. A reader should be able to write the ads from this file alone.
