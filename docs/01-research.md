# 01 · Research (2 to 3 hours)

Research is reading what already runs, not brainstorming. Do it in this order.

1. **Your own category rules.** If you keep a vault of winning ads, read the category first: the price shapes, the destinations, the longest runners. If you do not, start one after this launch.
2. **Meta Ad Library, three sweeps.** (a) the offer keyword + the metro, (b) national chains in the category, (c) same-avatar adjacent offers (for body contouring: cryo, red light, Emsculpt; for resurfacing: peels, microneedling, IPL, tox bundles). Log 20+ ads: advertiser, hook, offer shape, format, CTA, destination, days running, number of variants. **Days running and variant count are the winner signals.** For video, use the Apify recipe in `tools/adlibrary-scrape.md`; it returns the video files and preview images.
3. **Account history.** The client's last 90 days at ad level, ranked by cost per lead on the real conversion. Anything under your "excellent" band is a proven reusable and goes in the remix pack.
4. **Customer language.** Ten lines minimum in the patient's own words. Sources: the client's inbox and reviews; for a new offer with no history, Reddit threads (recipe in `tools/reddit-voc.md`), RealSelf, competitor ad comments. Capture verbatim. Replace with real inbox lines at the week-3 refresh.
5. **The competitor with your device.** Search the device name. If a distributor or a pop-up runs it in your city, that is your first positioning problem and your first angle ("same device, the difference is who assesses you").

Output: ICP and the beliefs she holds, positioning stack (mechanism → honesty → identity → offer), open lanes, competitor table, customer-language bank, segments. A reader should be able to write the ads from this file alone.
