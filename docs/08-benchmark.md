# 08 · Benchmark against the library (45 minutes)

Run a fresh ACTIVE sweep of the offer and the same-avatar offers. Build a table of what is live (variant count is the investment signal). Then score yourself against it on: hook psychology, price shape, proof, format mix, language (Spanish is a free lane in most US metros), program framing, urgency, local qualifier, copy density, funnel readiness.

Give two numbers: creative out of 10, converting system out of 10. They are different. A beautiful set with no price shape, no proof and a broken pixel is an 8 and a 5.

End with ranked fixes and a do-not-copy list (percent-off, "free consult", result claims, buy-now for physician-decided treatments).
