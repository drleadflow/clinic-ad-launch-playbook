# 04 · Creative strategy and framework (1 hour)

`09-creative-strategy.md` answers, per segment: awareness level (unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware, most-aware) → ad objective (awareness, converting, retargeting) → framework → static format → cell in the 12-cell wave-1 matrix (four groups × three cells).

Static formats that work in this category: Offer Card (skeleton with a price shape), Editorial Callout (her sentence, small physician answer), Side-by-Side (fair comparison), Notes screenshot (native, first person), Forum card ("what the forums say", no logos or usernames), Golden Review (only with a real review), Native phone photo (device in use, bright, city + price).

`06-creative-framework.md` fixes the skeleton for this offer. The proven med-spa skeleton, top to bottom: local strip ("NOW AVAILABLE IN <CITY>"), procedure or area in display type, one price shape (starting at / dollars off / strike-through), four compliant benefit bullets, dashed treatment-zone lines on the patient, CTA pill, address bar. Keep editorial photography inside it; that is what separates you from the template floor.
