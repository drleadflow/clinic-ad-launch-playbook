# 05 · Copy (2 hours)

One primary text and one headline per creative, organised by family (one angle per family, two to three variants). Hook in the first 125 characters. Headline under 40 characters. Description carries the credential or the deposit line.

Rules that came from real failures:
- No apostrophes in large on-image headlines (image models render "ISN'T" as "IN'T"); write "IS NOT".
- Zero em dashes anywhere client-facing.
- A monthly payment figure is a triggering term: it needs the example amount, the term and the APR in the same unit, or do not print it.
- Deposit wording exactly as the practice uses it (refundable? credited?).
- Never "permanent", "melt", "erase", "lose X inches", "safe for all skin types", "FDA-cleared for <indication>" unless the clearance is on file for that indication.
- The patient's sentence is the headline. The physician's answer is small.

Anti-slop gate before render: no stolen phrase over five words; "we" under a third of the "you" count; one contrast cliché maximum per family.
