# 02 · Remix pack (1 hour)

Pick 6 to 10 ads from research. Mix: at least three from the category's long runners, at least two from the client's own history, at least one organic format that is not an ad.

For each: link or library id, advertiser, days running, destination type, then two lines:
- **Architecture we steal:** structure, offer shape, hook type, proof placement, format.
- **What we change:** our mechanism, our price, our city, our voice.

Rule: borrow architecture, never wording. Any phrase over five words matching the source fails the anti-slop gate later. Videos: steal the structure (opener, caption cut, walk-and-talk, founder launch, zone offer), never the footage.
