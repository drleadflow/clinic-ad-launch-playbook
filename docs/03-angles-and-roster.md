# 03 · Angles and the roster (2 hours)

Sources to mine, in this order: your category lessons and headline bank; the 16 scriptwriting frameworks (Myth Killer, Pressure Cooker, Shortcut, Face-off, Conviction, Bridge, Educator, Witness, Countdown, Demo Drop, Time Machine, Origin Story, Spark, Raw Take, Cliffhanger, Grit); the client brief; the customer-language bank.

Build `11-roster.md`: 40+ angles in six to eight groups. The groups that recur across every med-spa offer:
1. Fear and trust (the horror story she read, answered by who decides and how)
2. Identity and zones (the part that never answered; one ad per body area)
3. After weight loss / GLP-1 (the skin, not the scale)
4. Honesty the discount market cannot say (a course, said first; not weight loss; the day-by-day)
5. Mechanism (what each energy reaches and does not reach)
6. Comparison (vs the thing she already tried, fairly)
7. Price and offer (one price shape per ad)
8. Timing and season

Score each angle CTR 1 to 5 (will she stop) and BOOK 1 to 5 (will she act). Add a do-not-run list: the generic distributor line, percent-off, claims you cannot make.

The laws: patient is the hero; headline is her sentence; open on a belief she holds; one desire per ad; provider appears as a hand, a handpiece or a name line.
