# 07 · QA and grading (1 hour)

QA sheet first: text defects, duplicated price, provider-hero drift, skeleton missing, banned words.

Then grade every asset out of 10:

| Weight | Dimension | Question |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Scroll stop | Is the headline her own sentence, specific enough to feel found out? |
| 2 | Two-second clarity | Face, procedure, city readable without the small panel? |
| 2 | Belief bridge | Starts from something she already believes and moves her one step? |
| 1.5 | Booking pull | Next action obvious and low-risk (deposit, consult, "see if you are a candidate")? |
| 1 | Visual execution | Patient hero, skeleton intact, no defects, native where intended |
| 0.5 | Compliance | Nothing that needs a lawyer or the medical director |

Tier A (8.5+) runs as is. Tier B (7 to 8.4) runs after the listed fix. Tier C is benched or retargeting only. Wave 1 is twelve cells from A and B, three per group. Write the fix next to every B.
